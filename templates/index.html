How the prediction is made

Your answers are passed through the same pipeline used to train the model: missing values are imputed, categorical answers are one-hot encoded, and numeric values are standardized. Two extra features are then calculated from your answers — Support Index (parental involvement + access to resources + teacher quality) and Study Consistency (hours studied × attendance). Logistic Regression scores each of the three classes and the highest score wins, so the result can be traced back to the exact contribution of each factor.
